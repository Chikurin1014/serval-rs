# Graph Tab

![The Graph tab](../media/graph.png)

Each graph plots the labels shown in the data list
(see [Label filters](data.md#label-filters)) whose types it supports.
The sidebar toggle at the top shows the list beside the graphs.

## Adding a graph

Hover over a category in the bar at the top, then pick a graph.

![Adding a graph](../media/graph-add.png)

| Menu | Graph | Shows |
|---|---|---|
| Time series | **Points**, **Linear**, **Stepped** | Numbers against time |
| Statistics | **Box plot** | The spread of each number label |
| Statistics | **Scatter** | One number label against another |
| Vector | **Arrows (2D / 3D)** | 2D / 3D vectors as arrows |
| Text | **Log** | The logs |

## Settings

Click a graph's title to see its settings, such as its title, the scale of its axes (linear or log), and its time range.

> [!TIP]
> You can hide a label by clicking it in the legend.

## Pausing

The pause button stops the graphs where they are.
The data keep coming while they are paused, and the play button brings the
graphs up to date.

## Arranging graphs

Drag a graph by its handle to move it, or remove it with its button.
